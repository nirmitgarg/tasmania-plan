## **DAY 1 - Wednesday, April 2: Arrival & Launceston Discovery**

**8:00 AM**: Disembark Spirit of Tasmania at Devonport

**8:30-9:30 AM**: Relaxed breakfast at **Mrs Jones Cafe** in Devonport

- Sit down, enjoy proper coffee
- Let baby be fed/changed in comfort
- No rushing - you’re on holiday!
- Stock up on car snacks for the week

**10:00 AM**: Drive to Launceston (1 hour, easy scenic drive)

**11:00 AM**: Arrive Launceston

**11:15 AM-1:30 PM**: **CATARACT GORGE** (2 hours 15 min)

This is one of the world’s most spectacular urban gorges - right in the city!

**Activities:**

- **Chairlift ride across the gorge** ($18 adult return)
  - Kids will LOVE this - it’s thrilling but safe
  - Stunning views suspended over the gorge
  - 7-year-old will think it’s the coolest thing ever
  - Baby can go in carrier (one parent at a time)
- **First Basin walk** (easy 45-min circuit around basin)
  - Mostly flat, some stairs
  - Beautiful gardens
  - Stroller-manageable on main paths
- **Peacocks roaming freely** - kids can get close for photos
- **Playground** near First Basin - let kids burn energy
- **Swimming pool** (outdoor, heated, but might be cool in April - check if open)

**12:30 PM**: Coffee/early lunch at **Gorge Restaurant** (overlooks First Basin)

- Decent vegetarian options
- Beautiful setting

**1:45 PM**: Drive to accommodation (10 min)

**2:00 PM**: **Check in to 17 Aram Place, Newstead**

- Unpack properly
- Everyone showers after ferry
- Baby naps in proper cot
- Kids can rest, watch movie
- Adults organize luggage
- **This rest time is CRUCIAL** after early morning

**3:30-5:30 PM**: **City Park** (5 min drive or 15 min walk from Aram Place)

This is pure magic for kids:

- **Japanese macaque monkeys** roaming freely in enclosure
  - Kids can watch them play, eat, interact
  - Absolutely fascinating - you’ll stay longer than planned
  - Free attraction!
- Beautiful Victorian park with massive trees
- **Conservatory** with tropical plants (warm inside if weather’s cool)
- **Playground** - modern, excellent
- Open green spaces for kids to run
- Ducks in pond

**5:45 PM**: Return to accommodation, freshen up

**7:00 PM**: **WALK to dinner** (this is why Newstead is perfect!)

**Dinner choice:**

- **Stillwater** (5 min walk) - Upscale waterfront restaurant, exceptional vegetarian menu, worth the splurge for first night
- **Blue Cafe Bar** (7 min walk) - Casual, family-friendly, great food, more relaxed

**My recommendation:** **Stillwater** - celebrate your arrival properly! The waterfront setting at dusk is beautiful, food is exceptional, and after the ferry journey, you deserve something special.

**8:30 PM**: Walk back to accommodation, early bedtime

**Why this day works:**

- ✅ Respects everyone’s energy after 8 AM ferry
- ✅ Still sees two major Launceston attractions
- ✅ Kids have fun (chairlift, peacocks, monkeys!)
- ✅ Proper rest time built in
- ✅ Walking distance dinner (no more driving!)
- ✅ Sets positive, relaxed tone for trip

**Accommodation**: 17 Aram Place, Newstead (Night 1/3)

## **DAY 2 - Thursday, April 3: Cradle Mountain Expedition**

**Your full wilderness day - this is what Tasmania is famous for!**

**6:45 AM**: Wake up call, early breakfast at accommodation

- Cook substantial breakfast - you need fuel
- **Pack generous picnic lunch:** sandwiches, fruit, snacks, chocolates, water, thermos with coffee/tea

**7:30 AM**: Depart for Cradle Mountain (1.5 hours via Sheffield)

**8:00 AM**: Optional quick stop at **Sheffield** (10-15 min)

- “Town of Murals” - 60+ outdoor murals on buildings
- Quick photo opportunity
- Toilets
- Coffee if needed at local cafe
- **Only stop if running on time and everyone’s comfortable**

**9:00 AM**: Arrive **Cradle Mountain Visitor Centre**

- Use facilities (important - limited toilets on trails)
- Get park map
- Check weather conditions
- Rangers can advise on track conditions
- Have National Parks Pass ready on phone or printed

**9:15 AM-3:30 PM**: **CRADLE MOUNTAIN-LAKE ST CLAIR NATIONAL PARK** (6 hours 15 min)

**9:15 AM**: Shuttle bus or drive to Dove Lake (10 min)

**9:30 AM-12:15 PM**: **DOVE LAKE AREA** (2 hours 45 min)

**Main walk - I recommend the FULL Dove Lake Circuit for you:**

**Dove Lake Circuit** (6km, 2-2.5 hours)

- Circumnavigates stunning Dove Lake
- Cradle Mountain as backdrop (iconic Tasmania photo)
- Mostly boardwalk and well-maintained gravel track
- Some sections have stairs/rocks
- **Strategy with 7-month-old:**
  - Bring baby in carrier (front or back carrier)
  - Bring lightweight stroller for boardwalk sections if you want
  - One parent can carry baby in carrier, other manages stroller
  - OR just use carrier entire time (easier actually)
  - Many families with babies do this circuit - you’ll be fine!

**Why you should do the full circuit:**

- Views change constantly - each angle is different
- It’s the quintessential Tasmania experience
- Your 11 and 7-year-old can easily manage this
- Not too strenuous but feels like an adventure
- You’ll be SO glad you did it

**Key stops on circuit:**

- **Glacier Rock** (massive boulder, great photos, halfway point)
- **Boat Shed** (picturesque, classic view)
- Various viewing platforms

**11:45 AM-12:15 PM**: **Enchanted Walk** (20 min loop)

- Just 5 min from Dove Lake car park
- Through ancient rainforest
- Massive trees covered in moss
- Boardwalk, completely flat, stroller-friendly
- **Feels magical** - like stepping into a fairy tale
- Kids will be enchanted (pun intended!)
- **DO NOT SKIP THIS** - it’s easy and spectacular

**12:15-1:15 PM**: **LUNCH BREAK** (1 hour)

- Dove Lake day shelter (covered area, tables, toilets)
- Eat your picnic lunch
- Let kids run around
- Baby feeding/changing
- Rest before afternoon activity
- Enjoy the views

**1:15 PM**: Drive to **Devils@Cradle** (5 min from Dove Lake)

**1:30-3:30 PM**: **DEVILS@CRADLE WILDLIFE SANCTUARY** (2 hours)

**This is UNMISSABLE - the highlight for kids!**

**2:00 PM - Tasmanian Devil Feeding** (main event)

- Keeper brings out devils for feeding time
- Watch them eat (they’re voracious, make incredible sounds!)
- Keeper explains conservation efforts
- Tasmanian devils are endangered - this is educational and special
- Kids will be absolutely captivated
- Takes about 30 minutes

**Other animals at sanctuary:**

- **Quolls** (spotted marsaial carnivores, super cute)
- **Wombats** (adorable, chunky, kids love them)
- **Tasmanian bettongs** (small kangaroo relatives)
- **Birds of prey** (wedge-tailed eagles, owls)
- **Eastern barred bandicoots**

**Why this is perfect for your family:**

- Educational - kids learn about endangered species
- Interactive - keeper talks, Q&A
- All ages enjoy it (7-month-old can watch from carrier, 7 and 11-year-olds fascinated)
- Not too exhausting after morning walk
- Tasmania-specific animals you can’t see elsewhere

**Entry:** ~$40 adult, ~$25 kids, family passes available (~$110 for family)

**3:45 PM**: Depart Cradle Mountain for Launceston

**4:15 PM**: Optional stop at **Mole Creek** (10 min off highway, 15 min stop)

- **Mole Creek Honey** shop
- Local honey, ice cream
- Toilets, stretch legs
- **Recommended** - everyone needs a break after big day

**5:30 PM**: Arrive back in Launceston

**6:00-6:30 PM**: Rest at accommodation

- Everyone freshen up
- Baby fed if needed
- Quick rest before dinner

**7:00 PM**: **WALK to dinner**

**Dinner choice:**

- **Black Cow Bistro** (has vegetarian options, more casual than Stillwater)
- **Geronimo Aperitivo Bar** (tapas-style, good veg options, nice atmosphere)
- **Indian Spice Launceston** (if craving familiar Indian food)

**OR if everyone’s exhausted:**

- Get takeaway and eat at accommodation
- Absolutely fine after such a big day!

**Evening**: Early bedtime - you earned it!

**What you achieved today:**

- ✅ Iconic Dove Lake Circuit
- ✅ Enchanted Walk magic
- ✅ Tasmanian devil feeding (bucket list!)
- ✅ Saw endangered wildlife
- ✅ Experienced Tasmania’s wilderness
- ✅ Kids had adventure of a lifetime

**Accommodation**: 17 Aram Place, Newstead (Night 2/3)

## **DAY 3 - Friday, April 4: Tamar Valley Discovery**

**More relaxed day after yesterday’s big adventure**

**8:30 AM**: Leisurely breakfast

**Options:**

- **Walk to Bread & Butter** (10 min) - excellent breakfast/brunch
- **Walk to Blue Cafe Bar** (7 min) - great coffee, good food
- Cook at accommodation if you prefer

**9:30 AM**: Depart for Tamar Valley (30-45 min drive north to Beauty Point)

**10:15 AM-12:30 PM**: **BEAUTY POINT ATTRACTIONS** (2 hours 15 min)

**10:15-11:15 AM: Seahorse World** (1 hour)

**World’s only seahorse aquarium breeding center:**

- See seahorses at all life stages (eggs to adults)
- **Feeding demonstrations** - fascinating to watch
- Touch tanks - kids can touch sea stars, other marine life
- Learn about seahorse biology (males give birth!)
- Educational and unique
- Entry: ~$25 adult, ~$20 kids (5-15), under 5 free
- Family pass: ~$70

**Why kids will love this:**

- Seahorses are magical-looking creatures
- Baby seahorses are incredibly tiny and cute
- Interactive elements
- Not too long (perfect attention span for 7-year-old)

**11:30 AM-12:15 PM: Platypus House** (45 min)

**Right next door to Seahorse World:**

- See **platypuses swimming** - rare opportunity!
- They’re active, swimming, diving for food
- Also **echidnas** (spiny anteaters - only other egg-laying mammal)
- Viewing tanks allow close observation
- Entry: ~$18 adult, ~$15 kids
- **Combined ticket** with Seahorse World saves money

**Why this is special:**

- Platypuses are notoriously hard to see in wild
- Fascinating creatures (monotremes - egg-laying mammals)
- Kids will be amazed by how weird they look!
- Educational

**12:30 PM**: Lunch at **Peppers York Cove** (waterfront, beautiful views) or **Moby’s** (more casual, fish & chips but has veg options)

**1:30-3:45 PM**: **TAMAR VALLEY WINE REGION** (2 hours 15 min)

**1:45-2:45 PM: Josef Chromy Wines** (1 hour)

Don’t worry - this isn’t just about wine! It’s perfect for families:

**Why you’re going:**

- **Stunning property** - beautiful gardens, lake, manicured grounds
- Kids can explore safely - open spaces to run around
- **Cellar door** has fresh juices, sparkling grape juice, coffee
- **Cheese platters** and produce (vegetarian-friendly!)
- Relax on lawn while kids play
- Adults can taste wines if interested, but not the point
- Peaceful, beautiful setting

**Entry:** Free to visit grounds and cellar door

**3:00-3:45 PM: Brady’s Lookout State Reserve** (45 min)

**Short drive from Josef Chromy (15 min):**

- **Panoramic views** of Tamar River valley
- Short walk to lookout (10-15 min)
- 360-degree views
- Beautiful for photos
- Not strenuous - easy after lunch

**4:00 PM**: Drive back to Launceston

**5:00-6:30 PM**: **Final Launceston activities**

**Options:**

**Option A - QVMAG** (Queen Victoria Museum & Art Gallery):

- 10 min walk from Aram Place
- **FREE entry** (excellent!)
- Natural history, Tasmanian history
- Planetarium shows (check times - ~$8)
- Kids often enjoy this
- 1-1.5 hours

**Option B - Revisit Cataract Gorge:**

- If you want more time there
- Evening light is beautiful
- More playground time for kids

**Option C - Relax at accommodation:**

- Everyone rests
- Kids play
- Prepare for Bicheno checkout tomorrow

**My recommendation:** Option C - rest day before big travel day tomorrow.

**7:00 PM**: **Special dinner** - walk to **Stillwater** (if you didn’t go Day 1) or **Mudbar & Restaurant** (waterfront)

**Evening**: **Pack everything for Bicheno checkout tomorrow**

- Full pack tonight
- Load cars tomorrow morning
- Makes checkout smooth

**Accommodation**: 17 Aram Place, Newstead (Night 3/3)

## **DAY 4 - Saturday, April 5: Launceston → Bay of Fires → Bicheno**

**Checkout day, travel day, but with spectacular stops!**

**8:00 AM**: Breakfast at accommodation (cook - no time for cafe)

**9:00 AM**: **CHECKOUT from 17 Aram Place** (before 10 AM)

- Cars loaded
- Final check of accommodation
- Say goodbye to Launceston

**9:15 AM**: Depart for Bicheno via northern route (taking scenic Bay of Fires route)

**10:15-11:00 AM**: **BRIDESTOWE LAVENDER FARM** (45 min stop - 1 hour from Launceston)

**Tasmania’s famous lavender farm:**

- Beautiful even outside peak bloom (peak is December-January)
- Walk through lavender fields
- **Cafe** - excellent morning tea
  - Lavender scones (famous!)
  - Coffee, hot chocolate
  - Beautiful views
- **Shop** - lavender products (soaps, oils, honey)
- Kids can run around safely
- Toilets, facilities

**Why stop here:**

- Breaks up drive
- Beautiful photo opportunity
- Morning tea in lovely setting
- Kids enjoy the open space
- Unique Tasmania experience

**Entry:** Free to walk grounds, cafe/shop purchases separate

**11:15 AM**: Continue to Bay of Fires (1 hour drive)

**12:15-2:30 PM**: **BAY OF FIRES** (2 hours 15 min - THE MAIN EVENT)

**One of the world’s most beautiful beaches!**

**Main stop: Binalong Bay**

**What makes Bay of Fires special:**

- **Orange-lichen-covered granite boulders** on pristine white sand
- Bright orange rocks against turquoise water - unbelievably photogenic
- Crystal clear water
- **The color contrast is surreal** - looks photoshopped but totally real!

**Activities:**

- **Beach walks** along the shore (easy, flat, beautiful)
- **Rock pools** - kids will love exploring
  - 7-year-old can search for crabs, shells
  - Safe, shallow pools
  - Fascinating marine life
- **Picnic lunch on the beach**
  - BYO from Launceston or grab supplies in St Helens (15 min before Bay of Fires)
  - **The Gardens** picnic area has tables, BBQs, toilets
  - Covered shelters if weather turns
- **Photos** - this is Instagram-famous scenery
- **Kids can run and play** - safe, beautiful beach

**Alternative spots if you have time/energy:**

- **Cosy Corner Beach** (5 min drive, more sheltered)
- **The Gardens** (excellent facilities, multiple beaches)

**My recommendation:** Spend full 2 hours+ at Binalong Bay - it’s worth it! Don’t rush this stop.

**2:45 PM**: Drive to Bicheno (45 min south)

**3:30 PM**: Arrive Bicheno

**Perfect timing - check-in is after 2 PM and you’re arriving 3:30 PM!**

**3:30 PM**: **CHECK INTO BICHENO AIRBNB**

- Unpack
- Everyone settles in
- Baby naps if needed

**4:30-6:30 PM**: **Explore Bicheno town** (2 hours)

**Walking distance from most accommodations:**

**Bicheno Blowhole** (5 min walk from town center):

- Natural rock formation
- Water shoots through hole when waves hit
- Most impressive when seas are rough
- Easy walk on paved path
- Kids think it’s cool
- 15 min visit

**Whalers Lookout** (10 min walk):

- Short walk up to lookout platform
- **Stunning sunset views** over Bicheno and ocean
- 360-degree views
- Perfect timing for sunset (around 6:15 PM in early April)
- Great for photos

**Bicheno foreshore walk**:

- Along waterfront
- Easy, flat, stroller-friendly
- Beautiful in evening light
- See boats in harbor

**7:00 PM**: Dinner options

**Choice:**

- **Sea Life Centre Cafe** - casual, on waterfront
- **Pasini’s** - Italian, good vegetarian options
- **Cook at Airbnb** - you might prefer this after travel day

**My recommendation:** Eat at accommodation tonight - you’ve been traveling, everyone’s tired, cooking is easier with 7-month-old.

**8:45 PM**: **BICHENO PENGUIN TOURS** (must-book ahead!)

**THIS IS MAGIC - one of the highlights of your entire trip!**

**What happens:**

- Meet at tour office in town
- Small group (limited numbers)
- Walk to beach with ranger/guide
- Sit on rocks/sand at dusk
- **Little penguins waddle ashore from ocean** after day of fishing
- They return to burrows in the dunes
- See them up close (you can’t approach, but they walk past you!)
- Adorable, waddling, making cute sounds
- Takes about 1 hour

**Why kids will LOVE this:**

- Wild penguins! (Little penguins, world’s smallest penguin species)
- They’re incredibly cute
- Watching them waddle up beach is hilarious
- Educational - learn about penguin behavior
- Happens at dusk - special magical atmosphere

**Practical details:**

- **MUST BOOK AHEAD:** [bichenopenguintours.com.au](http://bichenopenguintours.com.au)
- Price: ~$35 adult, ~$20 kids (5-15), under 5 free
- **Dress warmly!** - can be very cold on beach at night
- Bring: warm jackets, beanies, blankets
- No flash photography (disturbs penguins)
- No guaranteed sightings, but usually see many

**10:00 PM**: Return to accommodation, bedtime

**What you achieved today:**

- ✅ Moved from Launceston to Bicheno smoothly
- ✅ Saw lavender farm
- ✅ Experienced Bay of Fires (world-class beach)
- ✅ Explored Bicheno town
- ✅ Saw wild penguins waddle ashore!

**Accommodation**: Bicheno (Night 1/3)

## **DAY 5 - Sunday, April 6: Freycinet National Park**

**THE East Coast crown jewel - Wineglass Bay day!**

**8:00 AM**: Breakfast options

**Choice:**

- **Bicheno Bistro & Bar** - walk from most accommodations
- Cook at Airbnb (recommended - earlier start, packed lunch)

**My recommendation:** Cook breakfast, pack substantial picnic lunch - you’ll want full day at Freycinet without restaurant timing constraints.

**9:00 AM**: Drive to Freycinet National Park (30 min south)

**9:30 AM-4:30 PM**: **FREYCINET NATIONAL PARK** (7 hours - FULL DAY)

**One of Tasmania’s most beautiful places - prepare to be amazed!**

**9:30 AM**: Arrive Freycinet Visitor Centre

- Use facilities
- Get map
- Check conditions
- Park entry included in your National Parks Pass

**9:45 AM-11:45 AM**: **WINEGLASS BAY LOOKOUT** (2 hours)

**The walk:**

- 1.5 hours return (3km)
- Uphill climb - 545 steps plus track
- Moderate-challenging difficulty
- **THE view from top is one of Australia’s most iconic**
- Perfect crescent of white sand, turquoise water, mountains
- Absolutely worth every step!

**Strategy with 7-month-old baby:**

**Option A - Adults take turns:**

- Parent 1 goes up with 11 & 7-year-old (45 min up, 30 min down)
- Parent 2 stays at car park with baby
  - Can do short walk to Hazards Beach car park (flat, 10 min)
  - Or just rest with baby
- Swap if other parent wants to see view

**Option B - One parent carries baby up:**

- Baby in carrier (front or back pack)
- Definitely doable - many people do this
- Carrier is actually easier than you think
- Baby can nap in carrier
- You all see the view together!

**My recommendation:** Option B if baby’s in good mood. The view is SO spectacular, and the memory of your whole family at the top will be worth it. Plus your 7 and 11-year-old will be so proud to make it to the top!

**The view at top:**

- **Wineglass Bay lookout platform** - spacious, safe railings
- Perfect crescent bay below
- Mountains surrounding
- Often see dolphins, whales in bay
- Photos that will make everyone jealous!
- Rest before walking back down

**12:00 PM**: Drive to southern park areas (10 min)

**12:15-2:30 PM**: **HONEYMOON BAY & SLEEPY BAY** (2 hours 15 min)

**Both accessed from same car park:**

**Honeymoon Bay** (5 min walk from car park):

- Sheltered, calm bay
- White sand, clear turquoise water
- Perfect for picnic lunch
- Tables, some shelter
- **Picnic lunch here** - beautiful setting!
- Kids can explore beach while you eat
- Too cold for swimming (April ~14°C water) but paddling possible

**Sleepy Bay** (10 min walk from car park):

- More dramatic than Honeymoon Bay
- **Massive granite boulders** - spectacular!
- Dramatic rock formations
- More exposed, wild feeling
- Excellent for photos
- Kids love climbing on rocks (supervised!)

**Let kids explore rock pools:**

- Safe, shallow pools
- Find crabs, shells, sea stars
- 7-year-old will love this
- Educational and fun

**2:45-3:15 PM**: **CAPE TOURVILLE LIGHTHOUSE CIRCUIT** (30 min)

**Drive 5 min to Cape Tourville:**

**Perfect after lunch:**

- **Fully accessible boardwalk** - stroller-friendly!
- 20-minute loop circuit
- **360-degree coastal views**
- Dramatic cliffs dropping to ocean
- Lighthouse at cape (can’t enter, but beautiful setting)
- Not strenuous - perfect easy walk
- Spectacular scenery for minimal effort

**Why this is perfect:**

- Easy after morning’s Wineglass Bay effort
- Baby can be in stroller entire time
- Views are stunning
- Quick but memorable

**3:30-4:15 PM**: **FRIENDLY BEACHES** (45 min - OPTIONAL)

**15 min drive north from Cape Tourville:**

**Only do this if:**

- Everyone still has energy
- Weather is good
- You want more beach time

**What’s here:**

- Long, wild, beautiful beach stretching kilometers
- Often see wallabies grazing on beach/dunes!
- Great for kids to run around
- Burns energy before car ride back
- Less developed than other Freycinet beaches

**My recommendation:** Do it if energy’s good - the wallaby sightings are worth it! But skip if anyone’s tired.

**4:30 PM**: Return to Bicheno

**6:00 PM**: Dinner options

**Choice:**

- **Bicheno Penguin Cafe** - casual, near waterfront
- **Cook at Airbnb** - relaxed after big day

**Evening**: Early night after spectacular day

**What you achieved:**

- ✅ Wineglass Bay Lookout (bucket list!)
- ✅ Honeymoon Bay & Sleepy Bay beauty
- ✅ Cape Tourville circuit
- ✅ Possibly wallabies at Friendly Beaches
- ✅ One of Australia’s most beautiful places

**Accommodation**: Bicheno (Night 2/3)

**9:45-10:00 AM**: **SPIKY BEACH** (15 min stop - 15 min south of Bicheno)

**Quick photo stop:**

- Beautiful coastal scenery
- Unique rock formations jutting into ocean
- “Spiky” granite formations give it the name
- Easy access from road
- 10-15 min max
- Stretch legs, use toilets

**10:45-11:30 AM**: **KATE’S BERRY FARM** (45 min stop - near Swansea)

**This place is BELOVED by families!**

**Why you’re stopping:**

- **Famous berry ice cream** (kids will love this!)
- Beautiful berry farm and gardens
- **Cafe** with berry-everything: scones, cakes, chocolates
- Strawberries, raspberries, blackberries (depending on season)
- Kids can see where berries grow
- **Morning tea in lovely setting**
- Shop with berry products (jams, sauces)
- Clean facilities

**Perfect mid-morning break:**

- Ice cream at 11 AM? Why not - you’re on holiday!
- Kids will remember this stop

- Small cells

## **WEATHER & WHAT TO EXPECT IN APRIL**

### **Average Temperatures:**

- **Hobart:** 10-18°C (50-64°F) - days mild, nights cold
- **Launceston:** 8-18°C (46-64°F) - slightly cooler than Hobart
- **Cradle Mountain:** 5-13°C (41-55°F) - significantly colder, alpine climate
- **East Coast (Bicheno/Freycinet):** 11-18°C (52-64°F) - warmest region
- **Mount Wellington summit:** Can be near 0°C (32°F) even if Hobart is 15°C

### **Rainfall:**

- April is autumn - moderate rainfall (~50-60mm per month in Hobart)
- **West Coast (Cradle Mountain):** Wettest region - be prepared for rain
- **East Coast (Freycinet, Bicheno):** Driest, sunniest region
- Rain can come at any time - always carry rain gear

### **Daylight:**

- Sunrise: ~7:00 AM
- Sunset: ~6:15-6:30 PM
- Good daylight hours for activities

### **Wind:**

- Can be VERY windy, especially:
  - Mount Wellington summit (extreme wind possible)
  - Bruny Island - Cape Bruny (exposed!)
  - Coastal areas
- Wind chill makes it feel colder

### **Tasmania Weather Reality:**

**“Four seasons in one day” is absolutely true!**

- Morning: Sunny and 16°C
- Midday: Cloudy and 12°C
- Afternoon: Rainy and 10°C
- Evening: Clear and 14°C

**Prepare by:**

- Layering clothes (add/remove as needed)
- Always having rain jacket accessible
- Checking weather forecast daily
- Being flexible with activities if weather turns bad

## **TIPS FOR SUCCESS WITH KIDS & BABY**

### **Baby (7 months) Specific:**

**Routine:**

- Try to maintain feeding/sleeping schedule as much as possible
- Plan driving during nap times when feasible
- Allow flexibility - baby’s needs come first

**Feeding:**

- Always have bottles ready
- Warm water in thermos for formula
- Bring more baby food than you think you’ll need
- Restaurants in Tasmania are generally baby-friendly

**Changing:**

- Most attractions have baby change facilities
- Remote areas (Cradle Mountain tracks, some beaches) do NOT
- Always carry changing mat and supplies in car
- Disposable bags for dirty nappies

**Sleep:**

- Dark, quiet accommodations help
- White noise machine maintains consistency
- Early bedtimes after big days

**Carrier:**

- Practice wearing baby before trip
- Get comfortable with longer carries
- Have both front and back carrier options

### **Kids (7 & 11 years) Specific:**

**Engagement:**

- Involve them in trip planning (show photos of places)
- Give them cameras to document their perspective
- Let them help navigate (read maps)
- Create a “trip journal” they fill in each day

**Energy management:**

- Let them run at beaches/parks between structured activities
- Playgrounds built into itinerary (City Park, Mersey Bluff)
- Swimming at accommodations if available

**Boredom prevention:**

- Downloaded entertainment for car rides (essential!)
- “I Spy” games with Tasmania-specific items
- Wildlife spotting competitions
- Geocaching apps (if signal available)

**Motivation:**

- “Carrot” system: “After this walk, ice cream!”
- Countdown to exciting activities: “Only 2 more days until penguins!”
- Celebrate achievements: “You climbed to Wineglass Bay lookout!”

### **General Family Tips:**

**Pacing:**

- Don’t try to do everything - quality over quantity
- Build in rest time EVERY day
- Morning activities generally better (everyone’s fresh)
- Early dinners with kids (5:30-6:30 PM)

**Flexibility:**

- Have backup plans for bad weather
- Be willing to shorten activities if anyone’s struggling
- Skip optional activities guilt-free

**Snacks:**

- ALWAYS have snacks in car
- Hungry kids = unhappy kids
- Snacks fix 80% of problems!

**Bathrooms:**

- Always use facilities when available (even if kids say they don’t need to)
- Remote tracks have NO facilities
- Bring toilet paper in car (some basic toilets run out)

**Photos:**

- Designate one adult as “photographer” each day
- Get photos WITH kids, not just of scenery
- Action shots (kids running on beach, climbing, etc.)
- Family photos at key locations (use tripod or ask others)

**Expectations:**

- Not every moment will be Instagram-perfect
- Kids will complain sometimes (normal!)
- Someone will be tired/cranky at some point (also normal!)
- Focus on the amazing moments, not the difficult ones

**Memories:**

- Talk about highlights each evening
- “What was your favorite part today?”
- Let kids call grandparents to share adventures
- These memories will last forever!
